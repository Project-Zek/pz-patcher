Dear Project Zek players,

Welcome to Project Zek! Below you will find our gameplay and client patch notes, with the most recent changes first.

** Discord and Community **

Discord: https://discord.gg/q7Gp3whxRH
Website: https://www.project-zek.com/
Patch archive: https://www.project-zek.com/news

- Check the playguide channel on Discord for client downloads, patcher instructions, and help getting started.

- Run the patcher from your EverQuest client folder to receive the latest client files before playing.

- Report bugs and other issues in the buggreport channel on Discord. Please include what happened and how to reproduce it.

- To link Discord, sign in to the website and open Account Settings > Discord > Link Discord.

- Team voice access requires a character at level 6 or higher on your linked game account. Access follows that character's Good, Neutral, or Evil team and usually refreshes within a minute. Deleting the qualifying character or unlinking Discord removes access.

Thank you for testing, reporting issues, and helping us improve the server.

- The Project Zek Team

September 16, 2026
------------------------------

** Rogue Poisons **

- Rogues may apply poison to daggers in both their main hand and off-hand.

- Poison now has a 10% chance to trigger on a hit, rather than activating automatically on the first hit. Each application has one charge.

- Applications remain active for 30 minutes and are retained when zoning.

- Use /poisons to see your active poisons and the time remaining on each.

- This system remains subject to testing. Proc chance, duration, and other values may be adjusted based on feedback.
